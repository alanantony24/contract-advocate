INDEPENDENT CONTRACTOR AGREEMENT

This Agreement is entered into between Acme Consulting Pte Ltd ("Client") and
Jordan Lee, Freelance Web Developer ("Contractor"), signed and dated August 15, 2026.

1. Scope of Work
The Contractor agrees to design and develop a marketing website for the Client,
including up to 5 pages, responsive design, and a contact form integration.

2. Payment Terms
The Client agrees to pay the Contractor a total fee of $3,000 SGD. Payment is due
no later than September 10, 2026. Payment shall be made via bank transfer to the
account provided by the Contractor.

3. Deliverables
The Contractor shall deliver the completed website no later than September 5, 2026.

4. Intellectual Property
All work product shall become the exclusive property of the Client only upon receipt
of full payment. Prior to full payment, all rights remain with the Contractor.

5. Termination
Either party may terminate this Agreement with 14 days written notice. If the Client
terminates without cause, all work completed to date shall be paid in full within
7 days of termination.

6. Liability
The Contractor's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total
fees paid, except in cases of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

7. Non-Compete
The Contractor agrees not to provide similar web development services to any direct
competitor of the Client for a period of 24 months following the completion of this
Agreement, within the same industry and geographic region.

8. Renewal
This Agreement may be renewed for additional projects. If the Client wishes to engage
the Contractor for a follow-up project, the Client must provide written notice at
least 30 days before the one-year anniversary of this Agreement (i.e. by no later
than July 16, 2027), or the preferential rate outlined in this Agreement will no
longer apply.
